Send it your own pixels

The wall listens on UDP and speaks a one-frame-per-datagram dialect of PPM. There is no handshake, no authentication and no session — a datagram that arrives is drawn.

Draw on a layer, not on the bare wall. Layers stack like transparencies on a projector: the #FT: x y z line puts you on layer z, above the demos, where black is see-through. Layer 0 is the one the rotation is painting sixty times a second, so anything you send there is overwritten before you can look up.

# a red square on layer 5, from anything that can write to a socket
python3 -c 'import socket
f = b"P6\n64 64\n#FT: 128 0 5\n255\n" + bytes([255,0,0]) * 64 * 64
socket.socket(2, 2).sendto(f, ("betelgeuse", 1337))'

A layer nobody refreshes is dropped after 15 seconds, so whatever you leave behind cleans itself up — and to keep something on screen you resend it, which is why the demos are a loop rather than a drawing.
